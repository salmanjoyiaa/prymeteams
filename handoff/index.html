FAQ

What's the difference between customer support and customer success?+

Customer support is reactive. It waits for a problem, fixes it, and closes the ticket.

Customer success is proactive. It understands your customers, anticipates what they need, builds real relationships and makes sure they get value from you — so they stay longer, buy again and recommend you.

Our experts don't just answer questions. They make your customers successful.

Will I get the same people every day?+

Yes. Whether you hire one Customer Success Expert or a full team, you get the same dedicated people, working only for your business. They never take work for anyone else.

Can I hire just one person?+

Absolutely. Hire a single dedicated Customer Success Expert, or a full team. Start with one and grow whenever you're ready.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on what you need — how many experts, which hours and shifts, and which channels. Tell us your requirements and we'll send a custom quote.

Can I see my team working?+

Anytime. Request access to our live office cameras and watch the people working for you, in real time.

What happens when someone is sick or on leave?+

A backup team member who's already trained on your business steps in. No gaps, no starting from zero.

Do I need to write the SOPs and training?+

No. We create your SOPs and training materials with you, so your processes are documented properly from day one.
